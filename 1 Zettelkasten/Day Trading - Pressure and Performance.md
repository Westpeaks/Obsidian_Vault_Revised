
- Pressure to preform can lead to you becoming emotionally compromised. 
- When you are emotionally compromised, you are more likely to make worse trades with lower accuracy results.
- It is about consistency on high quality trades, not hitting a quota of trades.
## Spiraling

- A negative feedback loop where a trader will start taking larger and larger loses.
	1. This usually starts with a surprise loss.
	2. Unexpected losses are part of trading. Do not try to make back the loss while getting emotionally invested. Be patient and consistent. Desperation is not your friend.
	3. Average losers (trading stocks) get bigger.
	4. Total losses get bigger as a result.
	5. Total losses can create a loss of confidence.
