## Why do you want to start day trading

- Day trading is more than just making money.
- I want to start day trading to work towards financial independence. If my wage is tied to a job, I will have to to be tied to working 40 hours a week for the rest of my life until retirement. 
- I want to see if I can change that. I may have a strong need for flexibility in my future. I would like to build a church. this will be difficult with a salary cap and the average time constraints of a 9-5.
- If I can make this work, I could change my financial future. I am willing to focus on the process to accomplish this.
