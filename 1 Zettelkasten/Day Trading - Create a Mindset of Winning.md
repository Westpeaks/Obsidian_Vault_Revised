## Consistency and high quality trades lead to a winning mindset

- Trading is a performance based career.
- You have to create consistent metrics to develop a winning mindset.
- Fake it until you make it. Develop an emotional detachment.
- A tier trades are the standard. Do not settle for anything less.
	- Identify a strong setup
	- Perform technical analysis
	- Choose the right place to buy
	- Adhere to your exit indicators
- Even with these you may only achieve a 70% accuracy.
